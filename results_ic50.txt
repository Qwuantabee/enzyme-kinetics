The conversion divides every value by the same factor, so it shifts
the distribution without changing its shape or the rank order. That
is the honest summary: Cheng-Prusoff rescales a series, it does not
reorganise it.

--- what this does NOT establish ---
ChEMBL's activity records for this target carry no [S] and no [E].
Every Ki above is therefore illustrative of the arithmetic, not a
measurement. To validate the conversion against measured Ki you need
a paper reporting both for the same compound under stated conditions.
That is the open item in README.md.
